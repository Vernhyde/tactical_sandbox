**Game Concept Pitch**

*Co-Op Tactical Survival & Squad Sandbox (Kenshi x STALKER x RimWorld)*

**The Core Concept**

*A survival game combining the deep character/gear systems of Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead and the atmosphere of Stalker with the freeform squad/base progression of Kenshi and RimWorld.*

**Key Ideas**

- Lone Wolf to Whole Platoons (Pawn Possession): You start in control of a single operator (Kenshi or Project Zomboid style). As you recruit survivors, you can directly possess any member of your unit or command them from an angled top-down camera.

- Autonomous AI: You don't have to babysit every character. Back at your base, unassigned personnel run on a RimWorld-style priority system (maintaining weapons, pulling guard duty, sorting supplies). In a firefight once an AI squadmate finishes executing your orders, their own independent system takes over, this can lead to them automatically seeking hard cover, suppressing targets you are flanking, tossing frags, or pulling behind a berm to apply a tourniquet to their own bleeding leg.

- Living World ("A-Life" Emergence): NPCs outside your area of influence are not static. Other factions, scavengers, and patrols move across the map independently hunting for guns and supplies, ambushing each other, and fighting to survive without waiting for the player. (Debating on the Depth, I can go crazy by randomly generating a bunch of stuff like Dwarf Fortress or leave at just low level Actions allowing NPCs to maintain their own needs and safety)

- Destructible Smooth Voxel Terrain (Enshrouded style): The world is smooth, but destructible. You can dig trenches and holes, building fortifications into the earth, and watch explosives carve real craters or breach building walls. Can even shoot heavier weapons through softer terrain

- True Line-of-Sight Fog of War: Scouting and recon actually matter. You only see dynamic threats that you or your squadmates have line-of-sight or awareness of. Once you scout an area, the static terrain/buildings lock into your "map memory" as a grayed-out snapshot meaning if an enemy patrol moves into a building after you looked away, you won't magically see them through the walls. This also means that if said enemy changes the terrain in any way you will not be updated about it's change till you are in view of it once again leading to changes in how your characters remembered things. 

- Co-Op Pacing: The Game runs in real-time during combat, with a 12:1 environmental day/night cycle (2 hours real-time = 24 hours in-game). During downtime at base, players can agree to speed up time, which instantly snaps back to real-time the second an critical action, gunshot, or unknown contact is detected nearby.
